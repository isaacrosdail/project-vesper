Radar Chart: Shape of My Life

Three layers explained. Asymmetric threshold polygon

  1. Layer 0: Maslow Minimum: Red dotted line in radar chart.
  2. Layer 1: 6-12mo performance - our "normal"/baseline. Lighter cyan color
  3. Layer 2: Recent (14d) performance. Accent-subtle color

The radar chart is the central insight of the app, a single glance at whether your life is in balance.
Each axis represents a research-backed domain of well-being, with the shape surfacing what data alone doesn't.
You should be able to intuitively SEE how balanced your life is.

  1. Maslow-informed asymmetry: Not all pillars are equal.
    Health crashing is existential, whereas Purpose dipping is fine for a bit.
    MM thresholds encode that hierarchy.
  2. Temporal layers: Your baseline vs your recent tells you direction. "I'm at 60% career but was at 80% career three months ago" is a signal.

Neglect Signal

EX: The idea: "Your Career pillar is thriving & your Health pillar is starving, and historically that pattern makes you miserable by week 3."

Cascade pattern that maps to Maslow: Health failing 

-> Rest drops (can't recover properly)
  -> Rigidity increases (stress response)
    -> Relationships narrow (no energy/confidence for people)
       -> Growth stalls (can't learn when depleted, also confidence-related prob)
          -> Purpose disappears (becomes a moot point -> in survival mode now)

This cascade IS the neglect algorithm? System shouldn't just flag health if that dips - everything downstream is at risk.

The threshold polygon on your radar would be lopsided by design - not a regular pentagon but a shape that's wider on Health and narrower on Purpose. That asymmetry IS the Maslow hierarchy expressed visually.

Rest/Recovery (MM: Safety - moderate threshold?) (downtime, leisure, fun)
  1. Quantifiable inputs: leisure time entries, sleep quality, days without work-tagged entries
  2. This is the counterbalance to Career - the app can literally detect "you haven't logged a single Rest-tagged activity in 12 days"
  3. My rigidity-under-stress observation fits here too - when Rest drops, openness drops, which tanks Relationships and Growth
  4. 3. Rest: Perhaps split physical vs mental vs "spiritual"? Find research to indicate whether this'd make sense even
Purpose/Meaning (MM: self-actualization, lowest min threshold obv) (spirituality, volunteering, reflection)
  1. Hardest to quantify, but that's actually fine.
  2. MM is lowest bc Maslow says you really only pursue this when the others are met
  3. Could just be a weekly reflection habit or journaling check-in. Don't force more here.
